About Us

We offer delicious Japanese cuisine right at the heart of Oxford Road. Many dishes have been created in house for our customers and cannot be found anywhere else in Manchester. We source fresh fish, meat and vegetables which are delivered on a daily basis. Our ethos is to provide an authentic Japanese experience in a casual dining location.
